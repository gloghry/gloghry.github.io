# Writing in Garett’s voice

## Approved baseline

The homepage introduction and About page were supplied by Garett and approved in conversation. Use them as the baseline for focused edits. The About page accurately distinguishes current C#/.NET application work from earlier embedded C, CAN communication, and operator-assist work. WSU study is at the application stage.

## Voice

- Write as a personal introduction to a colleague: straightforward language, contractions, and sentences comfortable spoken aloud.
- Connect work, interests, and previous experience rather than assigning each personality trait a separate paragraph.
- Use technical details when they explain actual work or interests. Do not make every example a lesson about character.
- Keep current application work and interest in firmware accurate. Do not exaggerate technical expertise or leadership experience.
- Avoid résumé language, slogans, and stock phrases such as “bridge software and hardware,” “cross-functional collaboration,” “passionate about,” and “real-world solutions.” Avoid equally vague casual substitutes.
- Make focused edits when a sentence needs work. Ask for missing personal details rather than inventing them.
- Keep private workplace troubleshooting incidents out of public copy. Interview answers are context, not blanket permission to publish.
